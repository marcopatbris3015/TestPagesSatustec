A cause most assessments miss

Sometimes the system isn't broken. It's just not being used.

Across manufacturing, food and beverage, agriculture, and logistics in Texas and California, a large share of the supervisors, warehouse leads, and line operators who have to use the system every day work in Spanish. When the original rollout trained them in English, the result looks like a software failure but isn't one: transactions get entered late or in batches, workarounds harden into habit, and shadow spreadsheets quietly become the real system of record.

We interview, test, and retrain in whichever language the room speaks, and write documentation in both.
